Saci is installed in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Reach the backend without exposing it:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "saci.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}

  export SACI_BACKEND_URL=http://127.0.0.1:8080
  export SACI_TOKEN=$(kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get secret {{ include "saci.authSecret" . }} -o jsonpath='{.data.{{ .Values.auth.secretKey }}}' | base64 -d)
  saci list

On OpenShift use `oc` in place of `kubectl`. See docs/helm.md.
